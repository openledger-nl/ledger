# MMM labels

The five Me & My Machine labels in colour, from the official label pack (`MMMLABELS-color/svg/128px`), renamed after
Ledger's levels. MMMLABEL is an initiative of Fontys University of Applied Sciences, WELGEEN and technofilosofie.com;
the labels are free to use in any context. What each label means, and the full pack: https://mmmlabel.tech

Used through `src/lib/mmmIcons.ts`; their colours are the `--mmm-*` tokens in `src/app.css`. Don't redraw them.
